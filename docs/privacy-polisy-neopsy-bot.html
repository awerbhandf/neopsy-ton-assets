About

ΝΕΟѰ bot is designed to help you replace harmful “brain programs” with useful skills and talents.

The idea is simple: you should not just remove a harmful pattern. Instead, you should replace it with a constructive direction, so your brain has a clear new path.

Description

The bot will help replace malicious programs of your brain with useful skills and talents. Once a day, spend 5–15 minutes and complete the ToDo list while sober. Go through the quest with switching wires and play games. You can also practice with prayer beads along the way. Set a daily alarm on your phone so that in the first days you don’t forget about the game.

How to use

  • Open the bot and read the instructions inside the app.
  • Choose what you want to replace and what you want to replace it with.
  • Once per day, complete the ToDo checklist (5–15 minutes), only in a sober and stable state.
  • Follow the quest with switching wires and play the games.
  • Enable a daily phone alarm to keep the routine during the first days.

Notes

  • This is a self-guided tool with a structured daily routine.
  • Do not continue the ToDo list during intoxication or withdrawal/abstinence.
  • If you have a serious condition or feel unsafe, seek professional help.
